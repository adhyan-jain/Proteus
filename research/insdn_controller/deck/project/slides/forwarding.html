Be upfront about what this is. It is a visibility study, not a detection study: benign traffic only, one switch, no attacks, no detector. The 4× comes with wide intervals (the 5-tuple runs vary a lot) because our learning switch also installs MAC-pair entries for ping and ARP, and those entries match later TCP between the same hosts and suppress PacketIns, which is why 5-tuple entries stay scarce even in 5-tuple mode. We present that as a design finding: in a reactive controller, entry granularity and idle timeout decide how much traffic an IDS can ever observe, independent of the detector. Cross-testbed detection needs labelled attack traffic, which is future work.
