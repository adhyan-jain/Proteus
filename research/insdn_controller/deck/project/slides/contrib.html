Contributions

1 · MEASURED GAP

Inside InSDN, the controller’s flow entries are MAC pairs or eth_type only, and it sees about one PacketIn per target packet in most scenarios.

2 · PIPELINE

A reproducible OpenFlow 1.3 control-channel parser and featuriser for InSDN’s 44 ONOS captures, with parser loss reported.

3 · DETECTION FROM THAT VIEW

0.946 balanced accuracy on unseen captures; the PacketIn-rate heuristic reaches 0.616; simple models suffice.

4 · EVALUATION LESSON

InSDN sessions are separable from background traffic alone (0.94), so report within-session, capture-grouped results.

Plus a fifth: a Mininet/Ryu study showing forwarding granularity and timeouts set what an IDS can observe. Novelty rests on “inside InSDN, controller-observable only”; LAN-SDN-NIDS (2026) builds a new dataset instead.

Claim carefully. We are not first to use control-plane telemetry: Arévalo-Herrera et al. (Data, 2026) released a new dataset with PacketIn and FlowMod metrics. What we add is the measurement inside the most-used existing SDN benchmark, its controller captures analysed for the first time as far as our searches show (searches are not proof), the controller-observable-only evaluation, and the confound quantification. Related work verified: Sarhan et al. 2022, Engelen et al. 2021, Pinto et al. 2025, Pekar and Jozsa 2024, Arp et al. 2022.
