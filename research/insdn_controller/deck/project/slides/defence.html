Is this just a better classifier?No. A linear model matches the MLP. The claim is the measured controller view and the evaluation protocol.
Is it novel?Within InSDN, controller-observable only: yes as far as searches show. Not first to use control-plane telemetry.
Is the session confound fatal?It inflates capture-level scores. Within-session, non-empty windows still give 0.906 per capture.
One testbed only?Yes. Next: a live Mininet and Ryu study comparing forwarding apps, with interleaved benign and attack traffic.

Limits: one InSDN testbed · strict labels leave brute force, web and exploit with 6 to 12 attack windows · no tree baseline · offline replay · live study is benign-only on one switch, so no cross-testbed detection yet.

Robustness, all computed: 5 s windows give 0.958 overall and 0.944 within attack sessions. Under the broader IP-based label rule the MLP drops to 0.881 (1 s) and 0.887 (5 s), still well above the best threshold (0.748 and 0.769), but Metasploitable-2 to OVS transfer no longer beats a threshold (0.678 vs 0.678).
