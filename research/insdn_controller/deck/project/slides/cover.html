What does the SDN controller actually see?

Controller-native intrusion detection on InSDN’s own OpenFlow control channel

Adhyan Jain · October 2026

Opening line: SDN intrusion-detection papers say the controller has a global view, but they are evaluated on flow features computed from packet captures at the victim host. This work asks what the controller itself observes, using the controller captures that InSDN’s authors recorded and released, and whether detection still works from that view alone. Everything shown comes from stored result files in research/insdn_controller/results.
