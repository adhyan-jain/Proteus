## Draft cover letter

Dear Editor,

We submit "Evaluation Validity in Software-Defined Network Intrusion Detection: An Empirical Audit of Controller Observability and Host Generalization on InSDN" for consideration as a research article.

Machine-learning intrusion detection for SDN is mostly evaluated on flow-record benchmarks computed offline at end hosts. Using the OpenFlow control-channel captures that ship with the InSDN benchmark, we decode all 44 ONOS captures (7,381,679 messages) and show what the controller actually observes: no flow-table entry or FlowMod matches IP addresses or ports, while an IPv4 punt rule delivers packet headers to the controller. We then show that a controller-side detector's 0.946 balanced accuracy cannot be taken as evidence of attack detection, because the benchmark records benign and attack traffic in separate sessions, and we quantify this with trivial-rule and host-identity baselines. Finally, we show that flow-feature accuracy of 0.9998 on InSDN falls to 0.907 ± 0.085 when source hosts are held out, with the dispersion consistent with which of eight dominant hosts is withheld.

The study is a measurement and methodology contribution, not a new detector. We state its limits explicitly: one SDN dataset, one controller, and a split-leakage finding that confirms mechanisms described by Flood et al. and Engelen et al. on an SDN benchmark. Every reported number is checked against released result files, and the code that regenerates them is released.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approved it. AI-assistant use is disclosed in the Acknowledgment.

Sincerely,
Adhyan Jain, Atharva Sheersh Pandey, Kshitij Manikshete (equal contribution)
